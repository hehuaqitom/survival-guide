## 🎯 Use Cases

- **Household emergency preparedness** — take stock of supplies, watch expiry dates, run the checklist regularly
- **Power or water outage / disaster lockdown** — find water purification methods, ration food, treat injuries
- **Hiking and camping** — identify edible plants offline, navigate by star chart, send SOS in Morse
- **Long road trips / RV life** — RV conversion plans, emergency numbers, radio channels
- **Learning emergency skills** — build survival literacy from scratch
- **When the network is down** — everything works locally, no connectivity needed
- **Long-term life after a disaster** — mini games, a piano room and white noise for sleep: small outlets for the mind

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download `mori_v2.0.html`
2. Double-click to open it in a browser (Chrome / Edge / Safari recommended)
3. That's it — on mobile, "Add to Home Screen" makes it feel like a native app

> No internet, no installation. Keep it on a USB stick or any device you carry.
